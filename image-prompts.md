# Image Prompt System

## From strategic idea to campaign-ready visual

These images were produced in two controlled stages:

1. **Scene generation** established the business narrative, composition, lighting, and responsive crop.
2. **Identity-preserving editing** introduced Kasim Aslam from an authorized reference while protecting the original scene, supporting people, and art direction.

The prompts below are presentation-ready versions of the production instructions. The language has been standardized for clarity without changing the creative intent or workflow used.

| Concept | Story | Website role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder Freedom | The company keeps moving when the founder steps away. | Hero |
| Founder Bottleneck | Constant founder involvement creates operational drag. | Problem section |
| Delegation System | Context and ownership turn delegation into leverage. | Method section |

## 01. Founder Freedom

![Kasim Aslam stepping away while his operations team keeps the business moving](assets/images/founder-freedom-hero-kasim-v2.webp)

**Creative objective:** Make the outcome of the offer visible in one frame: more founder freedom without sacrificing business momentum.

### Stage 1: Generate the scene

```text
Create a premium, photorealistic editorial image for a founder coaching program about reclaiming time from a growing business.

SCENE
A bright contemporary office opens toward a sunlit desert-city horizon. A calm, successful male founder in his 40s steps away from his desk while a capable operations team continues working behind him. The business should feel active, organized, and independent of the founder's constant presence.

COMPOSITION
Use a wide 16:9 cinematic frame. Place the founder on the right third and reserve generous, uncluttered negative space on the left for a large white headline. Keep the founder readable in both desktop and mobile crops.

VISUAL DIRECTION
High-end business editorial photography; candid rather than posed; realistic skin and fabric detail; clear morning light; calm, confident, forward-moving energy. Use crisp whites, deep ink, cobalt-blue details, and only restrained coral accents.

GUARDRAILS
The founder must be fictional and must not resemble a known person. No text, logos, watermarks, visible brand names, gradients, floating graphics, fake interfaces, handshake poses, exaggerated luxury, heavy blur, or dark cinematic grading.
```

### Stage 2: Introduce Kasim's identity

```text
Edit Image 1, using Image 2 as the authorized identity reference for Kasim Aslam.

GOAL
Replace only the foreground founder's identity. Kasim should be naturally recognizable, calm, and confident, looking slightly toward the left horizon as established by the target pose.

IDENTITY MATCH
Match the visible facial structure, complexion, eyes, smile, nose, jawline, and shoulder-length dark wavy hair from Image 2. Preserve realistic skin texture, hair detail, head scale, perspective, and scene lighting.

PRESERVE EXACTLY
Keep the wide composition, right-third placement, body pose, blue blazer, white shirt, dark trousers, tablet, office architecture, background team, desert-city view, depth of field, lighting, and left-side copy space. Do not alter any background person.

EDIT BOUNDARY
Change only the founder's head, hair, face, and the minimum visible neck area required for a natural identity match.

QUALITY CHECK
No blended identity, caricature, waxy skin, short hair, beard, facial distortion, mismatched head size, text, logos, or watermarks.
```

## 02. Founder Bottleneck

![Kasim Aslam working through an overloaded founder schedule](assets/images/founder-bottleneck-kasim-v2.webp)

**Creative objective:** Show operational overload as a credible business problem, not a theatrical stress cliché.

### Stage 1: Generate the scene

```text
Create a premium, photorealistic editorial image that reveals the hidden cost of a founder becoming the operational bottleneck.

SCENE
A modern, lived-in office in late afternoon. A thoughtful founder sits at a working desk with a laptop, monitor, notebooks, and a small number of realistic work materials. Multiple devices and unfinished decisions suggest that too much context still depends on one person.

SUBJECT
The founder is in their late 30s or 40s. They appear focused and overloaded, but composed rather than panicked. The moment should feel observant and credible, not staged.

COMPOSITION
Use a horizontal 4:3 frame with the founder on the left third. Include enough of the desk and office environment to tell the story, with a crop that remains effective on narrow screens.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Sophisticated documentary business photography; natural side light; realistic skin, paper, and screen detail; focused tension without melodrama. Use deep ink, warm paper whites, subtle cobalt details, and one restrained coral object.

GUARDRAILS
The founder must be fictional and must not resemble a known person. No readable screen text, visible brand names, logos, watermarks, floating notifications, fake interfaces, chaotic clutter, head-in-hands posing, exaggerated distress, stock-photo staging, or one-note dark-blue grading.
```

### Stage 2: Introduce Kasim's identity

```text
Edit Image 1, using Image 2 as the authorized identity reference for Kasim Aslam.

GOAL
Replace only the seated founder's identity. Kasim should remain in the established thoughtful side profile, visibly carrying a heavy decision load without looking distressed or theatrical.

IDENTITY MATCH
Match the visible facial structure, complexion, eyes, nose, jawline, and shoulder-length dark wavy hair from Image 2. Do not add glasses because they are not present in the identity reference. Maintain natural skin texture, hair volume, head scale, perspective, and late-afternoon lighting.

PRESERVE EXACTLY
Keep the seated pose, hand and pen near the mouth, dark sweater, desk, laptop, monitor, notebooks, coral bottle, office, skyline, crop, depth of field, and all lighting cues.

EDIT BOUNDARY
Change only the founder's head, hair, face, and the minimum visible neck area required for a coherent identity match.

QUALITY CHECK
No blended identity, caricature, waxy skin, short hair, beard, eyewear, facial distortion, mismatched head size, altered pose, text, logos, or watermarks.
```

## 03. Delegation System

![Kasim Aslam and an operations lead mapping a delegation system together](assets/images/delegation-system-kasim-v2.webp)

**Creative objective:** Present delegation as a collaborative operating system built on context, clarity, and ownership.

### Stage 1: Generate the scene

```text
Create a premium, photorealistic editorial image of a founder and a skilled operations lead building a delegation system together.

SCENE
A bright contemporary workspace with a large analog planning wall. The wall uses abstract shapes, timelines, cards, and color blocks without readable text. Two professionals stand side by side in active collaboration: the operations lead explains the system while the founder listens and contributes.

STORY
Show trust, clear thinking, shared context, and transferred ownership. The scene should feel like real operating work rather than a generic corporate meeting.

COMPOSITION
Use a horizontal 4:3 frame. Place both people in the middle-right area, keep the planning wall clearly visible, and leave enough breathing room for responsive crops.

VISUAL DIRECTION
High-end business editorial photography with candid energy, natural skin texture, crisp daylight, and an optimistic but disciplined mood. Use white, charcoal ink, cobalt blue, restrained coral, and pale green accents.

GUARDRAILS
Both people must be fictional and must not resemble known individuals. No readable wall text, logos, watermarks, visible brand names, handshake poses, presentation-to-camera staging, rows of people, fake interfaces, glossy showroom styling, or one-note blue grading.
```

### Stage 2: Introduce Kasim's identity

```text
Edit Image 1, using Image 2 as the authorized identity reference for Kasim Aslam.

GOAL
Replace only the male founder on the right with Kasim. He should be naturally recognizable in three-quarter profile, engaged with the operations lead and focused on the planning wall.

IDENTITY MATCH
Match the visible facial structure, complexion, eyes, nose, jawline, and shoulder-length dark wavy hair from Image 2. Maintain realistic skin and hair detail, correct head scale, perspective, daylight direction, and color temperature.

PRESERVE EXACTLY
Keep the female operations lead completely unchanged, including her face, hair, clothing, gesture, and body position. Preserve both poses, Kasim's blue shirt and hands, the planning wall, abstract materials, plants, office, crop, depth of field, and daylight.

EDIT BOUNDARY
Change only the male founder's head, hair, face, and the minimum visible skin areas needed for a coherent identity match.

QUALITY CHECK
No blended identity, caricature, waxy skin, short hair, beard, facial distortion, mismatched head size, duplicated people, changes to the operations lead, altered composition, text, logos, or watermarks.
```
