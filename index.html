The actual bottleneck

You do not need another productivity hack.

You need the business to stop routing through you.

When every approval, follow-up, and exception lives in the founder's head, growth adds pressure instead of freedom. The calendar gets fuller while the highest-value work keeps waiting.

  • Your team asks you to resolve decisions they should own.
  • Important context disappears across inboxes, calls, and notes.
  • Time off creates a backlog instead of proving the system works.

Established founders with healthy or growing revenue, an existing team, and at least 20 hours of lower-leverage work still flowing through them.
